About

Created by Engr. Lewis Baah

This app uses Leigon / Ghana Metre Grid (EPSG:25000) for metric coordinates and Accra / Ghana National Grid (EPSG:2136) for Gold Coast feet. These are historically different coordinate reference systems, not just one grid with different units. Confirm which system your office requires. For legal or high-precision survey work, use official control points and the transformation approved by your survey authority.
